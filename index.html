Who We Are

Our journey began in FLL with Alienators, where we took our first steps into the world of robotics. We then became Potato Heads, growing through new challenges, competitions, and experiences along the way. Today, we’re starting a new chapter as Arcnick in FTC. Looking back at these moments, we’re proud of how far we’ve come, from little dreamers learning the ropes to a team that believes in what we can accomplish together. We’ve learned that when we trust each other, collaborate, and believe in ourselves, there are no limits to what we can achieve.

System Specifications

Our robot uses an agile mecanum drivetrain optimized for high-speed tracking across the field. Key modules include high-torque intake systems and custom gear assemblies built for structural integrity during heavy game cycles.

  • Custom 3D Printed Intake Subsystems
